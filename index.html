A few things to know.

Are these rates available to me?

Rates in YieldScope are indicative. Eligibility, minimum amounts, currencies and terms vary by provider. Confirm the current offer directly before making a decision. The interactive examples on this page are fictional.

Does YieldScope sell investment products?

No. YieldScope is an information-comparison tool. It is not a broker, financial advisor or deposit-taker and does not execute transactions.

Can I compare a dividend yield with a deposit rate?

You can view both, but they mean different things. A dividend may change and a share price can fall. A deposit rate is subject to its own conditions. Similar percentages do not mean similar risks.

Do I need an account?

No account or sign-up is required. Your watchlist is stored locally on your device.
